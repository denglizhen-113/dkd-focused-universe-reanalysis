# v1.1.0 — M19 compartment-stratified systematic reanalysis

- Added reproducible GEO, ArrayExpress/BioStudies, and PubMed accession-discovery searches.
- Screened 322 unique dataset records and retained 11 Series from 9 source studies.
- Replaced historical manual gene groups with seven fixed Reactome pathways (783 unique human genes).
- Restricted the primary model to three independent glomerular sources; no cross-compartment pooled effect is reported.
- Added REML/modified Hartung-Knapp gene synthesis, two-sided joint-label maxT pathway tests, covariate adjustment, leave-one-out, correlation QC, and probe aggregation sensitivities.
- Froze the main result: 0/783 genes at BH FDR<0.05; three pathways meet the fixed replication rule; coagulation does not.
- Added the Scientific Reports manuscript, PRISMA checklist, figures, supplementary workbook, source archives, and validation tests.
